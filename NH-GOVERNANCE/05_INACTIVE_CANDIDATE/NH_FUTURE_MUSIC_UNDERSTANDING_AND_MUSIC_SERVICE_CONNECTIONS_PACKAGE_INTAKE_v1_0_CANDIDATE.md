## Ness request

Ness explicitly requested that N.H's understanding of music be added as future design work. This is separate from merely controlling Spotify or another music service.

## Preserved original intent source

This intake does not originate or replace Ness's fuller media intent. The

`NESS_DESIGN_INPUTS/NH_FUTURE_FEATURE_DESIGN_INTENT_FOR_LIVE_LOOP_v1_1.md`

In particular, its Feature Family A already preserves the combined experience

## Intended future scope

The future package must identify the still-open Ness decisions for:

- how N.H understands musical structure, style, mood, performance and relationships among works;
- how N.H explains uncertainty and distinguishes observation from interpretation;
- how music may relate to Ness's context, preferences and memories without silently turning preference into fact;
- what music-derived information may be stored, corrected, forgotten or kept local;
- how locally stored music and installed local tools are handled;
- how optional services such as Spotify may be connected, what each connection may access, and what requires Ness's approval;
- how the already-preserved inside-N.H media experience is presented — with
  Spotify and YouTube as initial examples — so Ness can browse, search, choose
  and play available media without leaving the N.H conversation;
- whether that surface is an embedded view of the service, an N.H-owned view
  backed by the service, or a deliberate handoff to the installed application,
  and how the source service remains clear to Ness;
- what "download" or offline availability may mean for each source: N.H must
  never claim it can copy, retain or bypass protection on music when the
  account, service terms, licence or technical controls do not permit that;
- privacy, third-party, account, subscription, provenance and disconnection boundaries;
- isolation between each music service and the rest of N.H, so one service never gains broad access to other tools, private memory or the computer;
- failure, offline, revoked-access and compromised-service behavior;
- later mechanical ownership for any local analysis or service connector.

## Boundaries

- This intake does not add an eleventh member to Bundle 7.
- It does not change, invalidate, pause or restart the active Bundle 7 review.
- It records no answer to the future questions and chooses no provider, model, music service, storage format or implementation.
- Spotify is an example connection, not the definition or exclusive owner of music understanding.
- Spotify and YouTube are initial interface examples, not adopted providers or
  permission to download, copy or retain their content.
- Local-first behavior and minimum necessary external disclosure remain requirements to be reconciled with the governing privacy and security design.
- Any future package identity, bundle placement, dependencies, acceptance, adoption, integration and implementation remain separate later decisions.
